Story
A rabbit in the spring took a walk out of his den and saw a massive field of blooming plants.
His friends advised him to not eat too many plants as they may bear fruit in the future.
The rabbit ignored this and feasted like a hungry hippo.
By the time of fall as his friends feasted on the fruits of patience, the rabbit had nothing but dry bark and a stock of grass. He starved and starved until he was a nearly skeleton by the next spring. 
From then on, he learned to cultivate his pasture and embrace the patience of God.
And by 2 years later, he was a wealthy farmer, growing fruits the size of Jame's giant peach.
