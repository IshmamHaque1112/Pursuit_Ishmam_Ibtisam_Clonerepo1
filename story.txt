Story
A rabbit in the spring took a walk out of his den and saw a massive field of blooming plants.
His friends advised him to not eat too many plants as they may bear fruit in the future.
The rabbit ignored this and feasted like a hungry hippo.
By the time of fall as his friends feasted on the fruits of patience, the rabbit had nothing but dry bark and a stock of grass.

After nearly starving through winter, the rabbit admitted his mistake and asked his friends for help.
They shared fruit with him, and he saved every seed while learning to cultivate his pasture and embrace the patience of God.
When spring returned, he planted the seeds and cared for the young plants instead of eating them.
Two years later, he was a prosperous farmer growing fruits as large as James's giant peach, with enough to feed every animal in the meadow.
From then on, the rabbit planted before he feasted and always saved a place at his table for his friends.

# I like how the rabbit becoming a farmer shows that patience and hard work can turn a mistake into growth.
